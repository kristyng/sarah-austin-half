Bridge: keep fitness until training starts

You've been running about 2 times a week, with a gap in September. Your goal for the next 8 weeks is simple: get to 4 easy runs a week and a comfortable 6-mile Sunday long run, so Week 1 of training feels routine. No hard workouts except one threshold test.

How to run the bridge

  • Consistency beats distance. 3 runs a week for 2 weeks, then 4.
  • Easy means easy: 10:45–12:00/mi, heart rate under about 150 bpm (Zone 2). It will feel slow. That's the point.
  • Long run grows 1 mile at a time, from 4 to 7 miles. Don't jump back to 11 just because you've done it before.
  • Strides from Week 3: after an easy run, 4–6 × 20 seconds quick and relaxed, walking back to recover.

Off days

  • Strength twice a week (Mon and Fri or Sat, 20–25 min): squats, reverse lunges, glute bridges, single-leg calf raises, planks, side planks. 2–3 sets each.
  • Walking, cycling or yoga are fine. Keep one full rest day.
  • Missed a run? Skip it. Don't double up the next day.
  • Sick or sore in one spot for 3+ days: rest and get it checked.

Breathing and heart rate

  • Carry your inhaler on long runs.
  • On cold or high-pollen mornings, take a longer, slower warm-up.
  • On days you use your rescue inhaler, your heart rate may read high. Go by pace and effort.

Ready for Week 1 when…

  • You've run 4 days a week for at least 3 weeks.
  • A 6-mile long run feels comfortable.
  • You've done the threshold test and updated your zones.

Threshold test (Wed, Nov 4)

  • 10–15 min easy warm-up.
  • 30 min at the hardest steady effort you can hold, on flat ground.
  • Average heart rate for the last 20 minutes = new threshold HR.
  • Pick a day you haven't used your rescue inhaler. A chest strap reads better than the wrist.

Checkpoints for sub-2

  • Weeks 6–7: 30-min tempo at 9:15–9:25 and 800s at 8:50–9:00 feel controlled.
  • Weeks 9–10: 3–4 mi at 9:05 with HR at or under about 170.
  • Mid-Jan tune-up: 5K around 26:00 or 10K around 54:30 means you're on track.
  • A bit short? 1:59–2:05 is still a big PR.
